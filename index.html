About

John McGregor works under the name McGregor-Codes, building websites, interfaces and the workflows that sit behind them. His background starts in a laboratory, not a text editor: a B.Sc. in Medical Biochemistry from the University of Cross River State, where he spent months running biochemical assays and keeping exact, audit-ready records at the University of Calabar Teaching Hospital.

That same attention to detail carried over when he moved into frontend development, UI/UX and graphic design, first informally, then through a Diploma in Graphic Design, and now through hands-on projects built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Tailwind CSS and Figma.

He's currently serving as a Corps Member with the National Youth Service Corps in Rivers State, where he was elected CDS President for the Federal Road Safety Corps community development group and built the FRSC CDS Attendance & Fine Tracker, a system his own team now uses to record attendance and manage lateness fines.

Automating the repetitive parts

Not every good system needs someone doing the same task by hand every week. The FRSC CDS Attendance & Fine Tracker John built for his community development group already does this in a small way: it calculates lateness fines and generates reports automatically instead of a person doing it in a notebook.

That instinct, replace repetition with a workflow, is the direction McGregor-Codes is building toward: connecting tools like n8n, webhooks and APIs so forms, sheets and messages talk to each other without manual steps in between.

  • ＝ Google Sheets and JSON as a shared source of truth
  • ↔ Webhooks and REST APIs linking one tool to another
  • − Reports and calculations that used to be done by hand
1
TriggerA form submission or scheduled event starts the flow
2
WebhookData is sent to n8n over HTTP
3
WorkflowRules, filters and formatting are applied in n8n
4
Sheets / APIRecords are saved and synced automatically
5
NotifyA message or report goes out with no manual step
